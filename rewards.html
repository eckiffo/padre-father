⚖   How Your Share Is Calculated

Your cut of the 50% holder pool is proportional to your Blessing Score relative to all eligible wallets combined.

Formula
Your Share = (Your Score ÷ Total Eligible Scores) × Holder Pool

⚡   How the Hourly Race Works

Every hour, one wallet wins. The wallet with the most activity points earned in that specific hour takes the full prize — no split, no 2nd place.

Hourly Score Formula
Score = (Posts × 25) + (Replies × 10) + (Likes × 5)
+25

+5

Prize Calculation (live from Dexscreener)
Hourly Volume = 24h Volume ÷ 24
Hourly Fees = Hourly Volume × 1%
Winner Prize = Hourly Fees × 10%
Example
24h Volume$240,000
Estimated Hourly Volume$10,000
Hourly Fees (1%)$100

✦  The hourly race is separate from the weekly 50% fee split. You can win the hourly race while also receiving your weekly proportional share.
