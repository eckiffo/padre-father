Drop your TikTok videos here as:

  padre-hero-1.mp4   ← first video (plays first)
  padre-hero-2.mp4   ← second video (plays after first ends)

The hero will auto-play them on loop, muted, in the background.
Videos should ideally be:
  - Portrait or landscape (both work, object-fit:cover handles it)
  - Compressed H.264 MP4 for best browser compatibility
  - Under 20 MB each ideally (keeps page load fast)

If no video files are present the dark hero background shows instead.
